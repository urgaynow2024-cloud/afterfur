18+ Social Media Built for the Furry Community

1. VISION

AfterFur is an 18+ social-media platform designed for the furry community, particularly artists, avatar creators, photographers, fursuiters, VRChat creators and other adult creators.

The platform takes the familiar social-media experience of Twitter/X and builds a dedicated community around it.

Users should be able to:
- Make short posts
- Share artwork
- Upload images and videos
- Follow creators
- Reply to posts
- Like posts
- Repost content
- Quote posts
- Bookmark posts
- Use hashtags
- Discover creators
- Send private messages
- Build their own audience

AfterFur's main goal is to provide a dedicated place for the adult furry community where creators can share their work without the platform being dominated by AI-generated content, art theft, spam or poor moderation.

The platform should feel like a real social network first, rather than simply an adult-content website.

2. THE MEANING BEHIND AFTERFUR

The name AfterFur is inspired by the phrase "after dark".

"After dark" is commonly used by creators to describe adult/NSFW content.

AfterFur takes that concept and turns it into a furry-focused social platform.

The platform itself should not be nothing but NSFW content.

Instead, users can choose what they want to see.

The platform can have a dedicated After Dark section for adult content while still functioning as a normal social network.

3. CORE PRINCIPLES

Creator First
Creators should have control over their profiles, content and communities.

Human Moderation
Reports should be reviewed by real human moderators. Automated systems can assist moderators, but serious moderation decisions should not rely entirely on automation.

No AI-Generated Content
AfterFur will have a clear policy against AI-generated artwork/content.

18+ Only
AfterFur is an adult platform and must have appropriate age-gating and safety measures.

User Control
Users should control the types of content they see.

Transparency
Users should understand why moderation actions were taken and have a way to appeal serious decisions.

Community
AfterFur should be built around genuine interaction rather than engagement farming.
